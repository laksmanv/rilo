# Rilo: Jungle Jumper — Support

**Rilo: Jungle Jumper** is a free one-thumb arcade game for iPhone. Leap from
totem to totem through a misty jungle — but every totem starts sinking the
moment Rilo lands on it.

- **Tap** for a quick hop
- **Hold** for a soaring leap

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. Plays completely offline.

## Frequently asked

**How do I jump higher?**
Hold your thumb down instead of tapping. A tap is a short hop; a hold is a full
leap. The longer you hold, the higher Rilo goes — up to a maximum.

**My high score disappeared.**
Scores are stored on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears them.
If you're signed in to Game Center, your best score is also on the leaderboard.

**Does the game collect any data about me?**
No. The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and works entirely offline. The
only optional network feature is Game Center, which is handled by Apple.

**The game won't load / shows a blank screen.**
Force-quit the app and reopen it. If that doesn't help, email me the device
model and iOS version and I'll sort it out.

## Privacy Policy

Rilo: Jungle Jumper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
High scores are kept locally on your device. If you choose to use Game Center,
score submission is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
