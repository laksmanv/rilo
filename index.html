Frequently asked

How do I jump higher?

Hold your thumb down instead of tapping. A tap is a short hop; a hold is a full leap. The longer you hold, the higher Rilo goes — up to a maximum.

My high score disappeared.

Scores are stored on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the app clears them. If you're signed in to Game Center, your best score is also kept on the leaderboard.

The game won't load, or shows a blank screen.

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If that doesn't help, email me your device model and iOS version and I'll sort it out.

Does it work offline?

Yes — completely. The only optional network feature is Game Center.

Privacy Policy

Rilo: Jungle Jumper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app requests no permissions and creates no account.

High scores are stored locally on your device. If you choose to use Game Center, score submission is handled entirely by Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, export, or delete. Removing the app removes all of its data.
